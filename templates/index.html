🪂 Mango Wind Hub Quick Start Guide
◉
Canopy Circles
The dashed circle is your maximum canopy reach in zero wind. The solid shape shifts upwind to show your real wind-adjusted reach — we display max upwind reach and min downwind reach. If you open in the shape you should make it back for a landing pattern!
✏️
Draw or Define Jump Run
Click Draw Jump Run, then click and drag on the map to draw the aircraft's flight path — OR — Define Jump Run with direction, prior/past, offset and length. An orange parallel line appears showing approximate freefall drift. A red plane animates the run, dropping jumpers at your chosen separation interval.
◎
Jumper Breakoff Diameter
When each jumper group opens, a translucent red circle (500 ft radius) appears, representing the maximum tracking distance a jumper can cover at breakoff.
⏱
Forecast Scroll
Use the Forecast Offset slider to scroll up to 72 hours into the future. Wind circles, drift lines, and all wind cards update automatically — great for planning a jump day in advance.
📍
Dropzone Selector
Switch between dropzones using the Dropzone dropdown. The map recenters and all wind data updates for the new location.
