Mango Wind Hub Quick Start Guide
◉
Canopy Circles
The dashed circle = zero-wind reach. The solid shape = wind-adjusted reach. Open inside the shape and you should make it back.
✏️
Jump Run
Draw by dragging on the map, or Define by heading/distance. Shows freefall drift, animates jumpers, and auto-calculates recommended separation based on ground speed.
🎯
Landing Pattern
Tap Place Landing Spot, click anywhere on the map, then choose final heading and traffic direction. Wind-adjusted legs are drawn at 1,000 / 600 / 300 ft.
✈️
Plane Tracker
Enter your jump plane's tail number to track it live on the map with dead-reckoning animation. Automatically detects and draws the last jump run when the plane dives after exit.
⚙️
Canopy Settings
Enter your canopy size, exit weight, and type to customize the circles to your gear. Defaults to a Sabre3 150 at 1.5 wing loading.
⏱
Forecast Scroll
Slide the Forecast Offset up to 72 hours ahead. All wind data, circles, and drift lines update automatically.
📍
Dropzone
Switch DZs from the dropdown. Map recenters and all data updates instantly.
