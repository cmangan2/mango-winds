Mango Wind Hub
Welcome to Mango Wind Hub!
Skydiving wind planning for jumpers, by jumpers.
💡 Tip: Add this to your home screen for quick access like an app! Tap your browser's Share or Menu button, then "Add to Home Screen".
Quick Start Guide
○
Canopy Circles
The dashed circle = zero-wind reach. The solid shape = wind-adjusted reach. Open inside the shape and you should make it back.
✏️
Jump Run
Draw by dragging on the map, or Define by heading/distance. Shows freefall drift, animates jumpers, and auto-calculates recommended separation.
🎯
Landing Pattern
Tap Place Landing Spot, click the map, choose final heading and traffic direction. Wind-adjusted legs at 1,000 / 600 / 300 ft.
✈️
Plane Tracker
Enter your plane's tail number to track it live. Auto-detects and draws the jump run when the plane dives. Freeze the trail to mark and share the jump run.
⚙️
Canopy Settings
Enter your canopy size, exit weight, and type to customize circles to your gear.
⏱
Forecast Scroll
Slide the Forecast Offset up to 72 hours ahead. All data updates automatically.
☁️
Cloud Forecast
Tap ☁️ in the header for cloud cover, estimated base, and precip for the next 8 hours.
